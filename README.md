# TikTok Safety and Violation Reporting Tool

A project focused on TikTok account safety, violation reporting assistance, incident documentation, and online safety awareness.

The goal is to help users organize information about potential TikTok policy violations and prepare clear, evidence-based reports through appropriate reporting channels.

## Overview

Issues such as impersonation, harassment, fraudulent activity, suspicious accounts, and other potential policy violations can affect TikTok users, creators, and businesses.

This project focuses on making the reporting process more organized by supporting responsible documentation and legitimate reporting practices.

## Key Features

Feature availability depends on the current implementation.

- TikTok Violation Reporting Assistance — Support for organizing information about suspected policy violations.
- Incident Documentation — Structured documentation of reported incidents.
- Evidence Organization — Organize relevant screenshots, links, timestamps, and incident details where supported.
- Account Safety Awareness — Encourage responsible account protection and online safety practices.
- Reporting Guidance — Help users identify appropriate official reporting channels.
- User-Friendly Interface — A straightforward workflow for accessing the tool's available features.

## Common Use Cases

- Documenting suspected TikTok impersonation.
- Organizing evidence related to harassment or suspicious accounts.
- Preparing information for legitimate policy violation reports.
- Learning about TikTok account safety and reporting procedures.
- Maintaining records of incidents affecting creators, individuals, or businesses.

## Responsible Use

This project is intended for legitimate reporting assistance, documentation, and online safety purposes.

It is not intended to facilitate false accusations, coordinated mass reporting, harassment, unauthorized access, or attempts to manipulate platform enforcement systems.

Users should provide accurate information and follow TikTok's applicable policies and reporting procedures.
